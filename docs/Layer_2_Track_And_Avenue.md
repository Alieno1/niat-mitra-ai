# Layer 2: Pick Your Track, Pick a New Avenue

**Selected Track:** **Track B — Bottom-of-Funnel (Lead to Sale)**  
**The Avenue:** **NIAT Mitra AI — Autonomous Vernacular Parent Closer & "Open Market" Lead Recovery Engine**

---

## 1. What the Avenue Is (And Why NIAT Isn't Running It Yet)
After a student pays ₹1,200 and qualifies in the **NAT exam**, NIAT's funnel enforces a strict **7-day window** (April–August intake) to move the lead from *Qualified Lead (QL) $\rightarrow$ Campus Visit/1:1 Interview $\rightarrow$ Seat Reservation (₹4,000 SR milestone)*. If human counsellors don't close the family within 7 days, the lead lapses into NIAT's **"Open Market"** dead-lead pool.

**The Avenue:** An **automated, multi-lingual AI Parent Trust & Verification Agent** (11 Indian languages: Telugu, Hindi, Tamil, Kannada, Malayalam, Marathi, Gujarati, Bengali, Punjabi, Odia, and English, with live Voice TTS/STT) that intercepts NAT-qualified or Open-Market leads before they go cold. Instead of generic sales calls, it lets hesitant parents interrogate **live UGC university approvals, exact state-matched student internship/revenue proofs, and transparent scholarship + ROI math vs. local Tier-3 colleges** in their native language, then books a Campus Visit automatically and syncs to the Growth Team's 7-Day Lead Pipeline.

**Why NIAT isn't running it yet:**
1. NIAT relies heavily on human tele-counsellors and commission-driven affiliates who cannot scale 24/7 across 11 Indian languages with verifiable, tool-grounded data.
2. Affiliates actively *avoid* transparent scholarship calculators because scholarships void their ₹30K–₹80K commission. An AI-native, commission-free closer solves both problems simultaneously.

---

## 2. Why It Fits NIAT Specifically
* **The "Dual-Entity" Confusion:** Unlike a traditional college, NIAT's buyer is a **risk-averse Tier-2/3 parent** paying ₹8L–₹18L for a model where *University X grants the B.Tech degree* and *NIAT teaches the skills*. Generic edtech chatbots fail here because parents demand proof of UGC recognition, hostel safety, and real local student outcomes.
* **Category B Campus Arbitrage:** NIAT pays up to **₹80,000 per enrollment** to human consultants for Category B campuses (NCR, Pune, Jaipur, Bhopal). Converting even a fraction of Open-Market leads via an automated AI trust engine cuts CAC by 80%+ while keeping scholarship transparency intact.

## 3. Hypothesis (With Numbers)
* **Baseline:** Suppose 2,000 NAT-qualified leads/month stall at the *Post-NAT $\rightarrow$ Campus Visit* stage (primarily due to parent skepticism on degree validity, fees, and Reddit/Quora doubts) and lapse into the 7-day Open Market.
* **With NIAT Mitra AI:** Deploying an automated vernacular AI verification & ROI agent via WhatsApp/Web within 15 minutes of NAT qualification will:
  * Engage **40%** of stalled parents (**800 parents/month**) in their native language.
  * Convert **20%** of engaged families (**160 families/month**) into confirmed Campus Visits / 1:1 Interviews, and **25%** of those into Seat Reservations (**40 incremental enrollments/month**).
  * At an average blended CAC saving of ₹35,000/enrollment (vs. Category A/B consultant payouts), this recovers **₹14 Lakhs/month in CAC** plus **₹3.2+ Crore in annualized tuition LTV**.

---

## 4. The One Experiment to Run First
* **Scope:** Take **500 stalled NAT-qualified leads** from **Telangana/AP (Telugu) and Delhi-NCR/UP (Hindi)** who have not booked a Campus Visit within 48 hours of qualifying.
* **Duration:** **14 days** (matching two 7-day NIAT attribution windows).
* **Execution:** Trigger a personalized WhatsApp link to the **NIAT Mitra AI Portal** pre-loaded with the student's NAT score, nearest partner campus (e.g., Aurora/Chaitanya or Noida International Univ), UGC approval proof, and a native-language Parent Counsellor Agent. Route 50% to NIAT Mitra AI (Test) and 50% to standard human follow-up (Control).

---

## 5. How We'd Know It Worked (Metric & Threshold)
* **Primary Metric:** **QL-to-Campus Visit (or 1:1 Interview) Conversion Rate within the 7-Day Window.**
* **Secondary Metric:** **Parent Trust Score Delta** (pre- vs. post-session) & **Seat Reservation (SR) Rate**.
* **Success Threshold:** The experiment is a clear win if the Test group achieves **$\ge 18\%$ Campus Visit booking rate** within 7 days (compared to a Control baseline of $\le 10\%$) with **$< 5\%$ refund/cancellation rate** at Term-1.
